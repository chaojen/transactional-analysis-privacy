# Privacy Policy

**Applies to: TA (also listed as "TA 溝通分析" / "TA 交流分析" / "TA Transactional Analysis")**

**Effective date: October 3, 2026**
**Last updated: October 3, 2026**

ChaoJen ("we," "us," or "our") respects and protects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data the TA mobile application (the "App") collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have.

By downloading or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account
To use the App you create an account with Firebase Authentication, using one of:
- **Email and password.** We receive your email address. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us. We send a verification email, and password-reset emails when you request them.
- **Sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple.** We receive an account identifier from that provider and your email address (with Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email, in which case we receive a private relay address). The provider may also share your display name and profile photo; the App only shows the photo to you and does not store it in our database.

You can link or unlink Google and Apple sign-in in Settings, as long as at least one sign-in method remains.

### 1.2 Quiz Results
When you complete a quiz, the App stores the result in your account:
- Your answer to each question and the resulting scores in the five categories
- The personality pattern derived from those scores and the version of the interpretation rules used
- When the quiz was completed, and an optional title you give the result

Only you can read your full results. While a quiz is in progress, your answers so far are saved on your device so that you can continue later.

A summary of your **latest** result (the five category scores, the pattern, and the date, without individual answers or titles) is also stored so that the friends feature can compare it (see Section 3).

### 1.3 Profile and Friends (optional)
If you use the friends feature, you can set:
- A **nickname** (required for the friends feature)
- A short **bio** (optional)
- A **profile photo** (optional). You choose a photo from your library; the App crops and resizes it on your device and uploads only that image.

When you set a nickname, the App creates a random **friend code** for you. Others can add you by entering your friend code or scanning your QR code. We also store your friend list and the status of friend requests.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in your nickname, bio, or photo.

### 1.4 Camera and Photos
- **Camera**: used only to scan a friend's QR code, on your device. No images from the camera are stored or uploaded.
- **Photos**: used only when you choose an image — a QR code image (read on your device and not uploaded) or a profile photo (uploaded as described in Section 1.3). The App does not access the rest of your photo library.

### 1.5 Usage Analytics
We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used, for example:
- Screens you visit
- When a quiz is completed, with its category scores and pattern
- Device model, operating system version, language, approximate region (estimated from IP address), and an app-instance identifier

We do not attach your email address, name, or account identifier to analytics data.

### 1.6 Crash and Diagnostic Data
We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports and error diagnostics, which may include the stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, and an installation identifier. Crash reports are not linked to your email address or account.

### 1.7 Advertising
The App shows ads through Google AdMob: banners on some screens, a full-screen ad after you complete a quiz, and a full-screen ad when you open the App (only after you already have at least one result). To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect:
- The advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS or the Advertising ID on Android)
- Device model, operating system version, language, IP address, and approximate location derived from it
- Ad interactions, such as impressions and clicks

**On iOS**, the App asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the IDFA can be used. If you decline, AdMob cannot access the IDFA and serves ads without tracking you across other companies' apps and websites.

**In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland**, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent message before requesting ads. You can consent, decline, or choose specific purposes, and you can change your choice at any time under **Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options**.

You can also reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device's settings.

### 1.8 What We Do Not Collect
- We do **not** access your contacts, microphone, calendar, or location.
- Firebase Analytics does **not** collect the advertising identifier; only AdMob uses it, as described in Section 1.7.
- The App has no in-app purchases, so we do not handle payment information.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Create and secure your account and let you sign in
- Score your quizzes, interpret the results, and keep your history
- Let you add friends and compare results with them, if you choose to
- Understand how the App is used so that we can improve it
- Diagnose crashes and fix bugs
- Prevent abuse and protect our backend through Firebase App Check
- Show ads through Google AdMob, which funds the App

We do **not** sell your personal data. Apart from the advertising data that AdMob collects as described in Section 1.7, we do not use your data for advertising.

---

## 3. What Other Users Can See

The App has no public feed or search. What others can see depends on how they are connected to you:
- **Anyone who has your friend code or QR code** (and is signed in to the App) can look up your nickname, bio, and profile photo, and send you a friend request.
- **People you have sent a friend request to, received one from, or are friends with** can see your nickname, profile photo, and a summary of your latest result: the five category scores, the pattern, and the date.
- **Friends** can open a comparison of your latest result with theirs.

Other users **never** see your email address, your individual answers, your result titles, your older results, your analytics data, or your crash reports.

Your profile photo is stored with a link that is hard to guess; anyone who obtains that link can view the image.

## 6. Data Storage and Retention

- Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers, which may be located outside your country.
- Your account, results, profile, photo, and friend relationships are kept until you delete them or delete your account (see Section 8).
- Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting of our Firebase project, and Crashlytics keeps crash reports for up to 90 days.
- Data saved on your device (your settings, an unfinished quiz, and cached data) stays on the device until you uninstall the App or clear its data.

---

## 7. Data Security

Data sent between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to the database and file storage is restricted by Firebase Security Rules: only you can read your full quiz results and change your profile, and only you and your friends can read your latest-result summary. Firebase App Check helps ensure that requests come from the genuine App. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

---

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

- **Results**: You can rename or delete any result in the App.
- **Profile**: You can change your nickname, bio, and photo at any time.
- **Friends**: You can remove a friend or decline a request at any time. This removes the friendship on both sides.
- **Account deletion**: You can delete your account at any time under **Settings → Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account, all of your quiz results, your profile, photo, friend code, and friend relationships (including the copies in your friends' lists). Analytics and crash data are not linked to your account and expire according to Section 6. If you can no longer open the App, you can also email us at the address below to request deletion; we will delete the data within 30 days.
- **Ads**: You can change your ad consent under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options (where required by law), turn off tracking permission for the App in iOS Settings, or reset the advertising identifier in your device settings.
- **Stopping collection**: You can stop all data collection at any time by uninstalling the App.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as to access or correct your data. To exercise them, contact us at the address below.
